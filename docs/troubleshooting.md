# Troubleshooting BirdCheck

Start with the symptom and avoid destructive actions until you understand which data source is involved.

## A bird I checked is not in my Life List

Check **Settings → Automatically Add to Life List**. A bird checked on another list does not necessarily prove that automatic Life List addition was enabled at that moment. Open the species in Life List and check its status.

## A city and a named place show different totals

Compare scope, period, and evidence. A place named “Nature Center” does not, by name alone, establish which city contains it. Verified saved geography can link a place to its city. When a smaller verified place has more qualifying birds than its containing city under identical filters, the inconsistency deserves investigation rather than guessed adjustments.

Include the exact question wording and a screenshot of the affected summaries, but remove personally identifying coordinates before filing a public report.

## BirdCheck AI answers a different question

Check the voice transcript for misheard bird or place names. Try phrasing the metric explicitly (“distinct species” or “recorded individuals”). Clarify whether you mean the **catalog** (“birds found in Texas”) or **your history** (“birds I have seen in Texas”). Follow-up questions work best after a specific question establishes the context.

## My latest Outing is missing

Look in **Outings & Sightings → Recent**, and confirm whether the session was finished. A Watch-started Outing can wait on iPhone communication; do not create a duplicate before checking. If the app cannot open saved records, contact support instead of reinstalling.

## A photo is not appearing

Open the species' **My Photos** section. Check whether the photo was saved, whether a different photo is starred as primary, and whether another device has finished iCloud syncing. The maximum is six per species.

## Backup/restore concern

Review the dates in **Settings → Backup & Restore**. A local automatic backup may lag recent changes briefly. **Do not use Erase All Personal Data or reinstall the app to resolve a display issue.** Restore is a recovery action, not a routine sync refresh.

## Live weather is blank

BirdCheck **build 8 removed the unsuccessful WeatherKit retrieval**. The app can retain previously saved/manual weather fields but does not fetch live Apple Weather data. This is expected for this version, not a missing WeatherKit permission to toggle.

## Health and location issues

Confirm BirdCheck and system permissions, device connectivity, and the Watch's own settings if applicable. Hiding a map does not itself disable route capture; denying location access may.

## Escalating to support

Include app version/build, device and OS version, the exact on-screen message, steps to reproduce, and whether the problem affects one or multiple devices. Do **not** post personal backups, iCloud identifiers, full GPS histories, or private logs publicly.
