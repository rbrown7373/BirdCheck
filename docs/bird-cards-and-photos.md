# Bird Cards and Personal Photos

Open a bird in a list, search result, or supported map view to reach its **Bird Details** card. Depending on the bird and your saved data, the card can display names, scientific classification, regional information, observation history, Known Locations, and personal photos.

## Six-photo gallery

The **My Photos** section sits **at the bottom of the bird card**. It shows a grid of **three columns and two rows** with a maximum of **six total photos** for that species.

- Your existing main photo occupies **one of the six**, not a seventh slot.
- Tap **Add** to use an available camera or photo-library flow.
- Tap a thumbnail to open that particular photo full screen.
- Tap the **star** beneath a thumbnail to choose that photo as the main image. One photo is primary at a time.
- The starred photo is used wherever BirdCheck asks for the species' primary thumbnail.

The gallery belongs to the **bird species**, not exclusively to one Outing or Sighting. Adding a picture does **not** automatically mean you have marked the species as seen.

## Full-screen photo viewer

The current viewer supports full-screen viewing, pinch-to-zoom, double-tap zoom, and moving around a zoomed image by dragging. At normal scale, horizontal swipes switch among the saved photos; while zoomed, dragging pans the current image instead of accidentally switching to the next one.

## Important: BirdCheck does not supply photos for every species

BirdCheck bundles reference bird **names and taxonomy**, but does **not** provide a complete photo library for every bird. Photos shown in personal bird cards are **user-added images**. Promotional screenshots should not imply that checking a species automatically downloads a professional bird photo.

## Photo backup and sync

The portable personal backup includes image bytes, species association, and the primary-photo flag. The CloudKit-backed personal store is intended to synchronize these records across the user's devices, subject to iCloud availability and synchronization. **Do not delete or reinstall the app to troubleshoot missing photos.** See [Backup and iCloud](backup-and-icloud.md).
