# Apple Watch and Fitness

BirdCheck's Apple Watch component can help record Outings and add birds without taking out the iPhone for every entry. It communicates with the companion iPhone app; some actions wait for an iPhone response.

## Starting a Watch Outing

On the Watch, begin a birding Outing. On a first walk, BirdCheck can ask whether to **Include Fitness** or use **Birding Only**. The Watch interface indicates that bird recording works either way.

During a Watch Outing the interface can show species and individual counts, steps, distance, an **Outing List**, **Add Bird**, and **End**. Use **Talk** or the available microphone control to record a bird by voice.

When BirdCheck sends a request to the iPhone, it may show a progress state such as **Waiting for iPhone** or **Checking with iPhone**. A request is not necessarily saved just because speech was heard; wait for BirdCheck's confirmation.

## Fitness and Health permissions

In **iPhone Settings → Record Outings to Apple Fitness**, you can allow recording of iPhone-started walks to Apple Health/Fitness. For a **Watch-started** walk, the Watch offers its own Fitness-or-birding-only choice. Bird recording does not require Fitness permission.

Step count and distance can depend on device source, permissions, and availability. Do not assume that the phone and Watch each have an independent quantity that should simply be added together.

## Idle reminders and completion

The Watch includes an idle reminder after a period without movement or a bird addition. It offers actions to continue or end the Outing. At completion, the app may wait while the iPhone saves the record. If a save is still pending, do not deliberately create a second identical record without first checking the saved Outing.

## If the Watch seems out of sync

1. Check whether the iPhone app is installed and can open its personal records.
2. Confirm both devices are connected as needed for the operation.
3. Give a pending save a chance to finish, then check the iPhone's **Recent Outings & Sightings**.
4. Avoid uninstalling or erasing either app to force synchronization.
5. If the issue persists, record the exact message and use [Support / troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).

The Watch and iPhone have different permission prompts. Enabling iPhone location or voice access does not automatically grant equivalent Watch permission.
