# Life List, Checklists and Regions

## Your Life List

The **Life List** records bird species that you consider seen. A checked Life List species is personal evidence of having seen that species, even if there is no dated Outing or Sighting.

The Life List is **not** an individual-bird tally. A species can be recorded once on a Life List even if you saw many individuals on several dates.

## Marking birds as seen

1. Open your Life List or another checklist.
2. Find a bird by browsing or searching.
3. Use its check control to mark it seen.
4. Open the bird's details for taxonomy, saved location information, observation history, and your photos.

Depending on the list, BirdCheck can also track a per-species **observation count**. These counts are distinct from the number of checked species.

## Automatic additions to the Life List

**Settings → Automatically Add to Life List** controls whether newly checked birds on another list are added to your Life List. Disabling it does **not** uncheck birds already on the Life List.

BirdCheck keeps track of whether a Life List entry was added explicitly or supported by an observation list. This matters when deleting records: an automatically supported Life List entry can be handled differently from a manually kept entry. Follow the app's confirmation carefully when deleting an Outing, Sighting, or list.

## Reusable lists and folders

Open the bottom Home drawer and select **Lists**. BirdCheck supports **New List** and **New Folder** actions, renaming, sorting, pinning, and organizing lists in folders. A folder must be empty before it can be deleted. Deleting a normal list removes its checkmarks and list observation counts; it does not automatically erase the corresponding bird species from the global catalog.

A list can have selected geographic regions and, in appropriate workflows, a recorded location or date. These details can help BirdCheck show relevant birds and interpret personal regional history.

## Regions and user-added birds

BirdCheck includes region reference data and a **Regions of Interest** experience. The **User Added** area allows creation of user-added birds and custom regions. A custom region is a personally managed grouping of species; it does not mean BirdCheck has observed those species there.

### Important distinction

- **Catalog occurrence:** A bird is known in reference data as occurring in a region.
- **Personal presence:** You marked it seen or have a saved observation associated with that region.
- **Dated observation:** A record provides a date/time and may provide a known number of individuals.

Do not substitute one of these for another. See [Counting and evidence](reference/data-definitions.md).

## User-created birds

Use **User Added → User Added Birds** when a bird is not represented as needed in the bundled catalog. User-created bird records are user-owned and are kept distinct from catalog updates. See [Bird cards and photos](bird-cards-and-photos.md) for personalization.
