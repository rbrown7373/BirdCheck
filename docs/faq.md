# Frequently Asked Questions

### Does BirdCheck provide bird photos for every species?
No. BirdCheck provides a bird catalog and lets you add **your own photos** to a bird card, up to six per species. The app's cartoon robin is branding, not a promise of a built-in bird photo library.

### Can I change the main photo?
Yes. Open **Bird Details → My Photos**, then star the photo you want as primary. That photo is still one of the six gallery photos.

### Can I move around a zoomed photo?
Yes. Pinch to zoom, drag to pan, and return to normal size to swipe between saved photos.

### What is the difference between an Outing and a Sighting?
An Outing represents a birding session and can include a route/activity data. A quick Sighting records birds without requiring a complete walking Outing.

### Does the Life List count a bird seen long ago?
Yes, if you marked the species seen. It does not automatically establish a date, city, or an exact number of individuals.

### Does a geographic name prove a species was observed there?
Only when the saved personal evidence supports that place. Catalog occurrence and personal observation are different facts.

### Why do bird counts and species counts differ?
One species may have multiple recorded individuals. Distinct species count identity; known individuals count recorded quantities from qualifying observations.

### Can I ask BirdCheck questions by voice?
Yes, with the iPhone voice-input preference and required Apple permissions. The Watch also supports voice-related birding workflows.

### Does BirdCheck automatically identify birds from their calls or photos?
This documentation does not establish a general-purpose automatic bird identification service. Do not advertise one based on the name “BirdCheck AI.” BirdCheck AI is primarily documented here as a **query interface** for personal records and catalog information.

### Does the app still use WeatherKit?
No. WeatherKit was removed in **1.0 build 8**; saved/manual weather fields remain.

### Are backups and iCloud the same thing?
No. iCloud attempts asynchronous syncing; BirdCheck's automatic/manual local backup snapshots provide recovery options. Neither is proof that another device already has every record.

### Can I share my Life List through a public GitHub link?
This GitHub documentation describes the app. It does **not** publish or share your private BirdCheck records. Don't upload personal backups or raw GPS history to the documentation repository.

### Does the app require Apple Health permission?
No for ordinary bird recording. Fitness recording features require additional permission.
